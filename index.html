⚖️ Live Character Budget Validator

Why character budgets? Canva text boxes are fixed. If copy overflows, text reflows and ruins deck unity. Enforce budgets before generating images!

Pillar Headline Budget (45–58 Chars) 49 chars · PERFECT
Format: The [Role]: [orange tool] [verb phrase].
Pillar Body Budget (65–80 Chars) 77 chars · PERFECT
Format: Crisp dark body line ending in ONE ALL-CAPS word.
Caption Hard Cap (≤ 500 Chars) 286 / 500 chars
Includes CTA on line 1, punchy body, and exactly 5 hashtags.
